## One-line version

- Blog: written for others, published when finished.
- Notes: written for yourself, continuously evolving.
- Projects: things you made, with status and links.

## How to decide

If you **want it read chronologically by search engines and RSS subscribers**, it's a blog post.

If you **want to find it three months from now and keep adding to it**, it's a note.

If you **want others to know what you built**, it's a project.
