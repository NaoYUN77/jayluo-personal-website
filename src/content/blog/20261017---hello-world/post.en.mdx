## What you'll find here

This site does three things:

- **Blog**: finished pieces that read well in chronological order.
- **Notes**: shorter, always-growing knowledge cards that are allowed to be incomplete.
- **Projects**: things I've built, with stack, status, and links.

## Why keep them separate

Blog posts are linear and organized by publish date. Notes are a network, organized by topic and status. Projects have a lifecycle — active, paused, archived.

Merging all three into one post list is convenient at first, but they start getting in each other's way.
